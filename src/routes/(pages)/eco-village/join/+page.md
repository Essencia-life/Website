## Join Essência Tribe!

### Sharing the Dream of a New Earth EcoVillage

*A courageous experiment about coLiving off-grid in Harmony with Nature and each other.*

### Our Invitation

We invite you to co-create and support a future with Essência, a vibrant eco-village community dedicated to nurturing sustainable living, personal growth and harmony with the Earth.

Nestled in a valley of the wild West Coast of the Algarve in Portugal, just 20 minutes from Aljezur, Essência is a living embodiment of a harmonious relationship with nature and each other.

At Essência, we are guided by the principles of permaculture, holistic living and collaborative community, offering:

- **A Living Sanctuary** — diverse eco-living space nurtured by renewable energy and the bounty of our vegan, organic food.
- **Pathways to Flourishing** — transformative retreats, workshops and nourishing community gatherings that foster creativity and joy.
- **Conscious Community** — a thriving social structure that demonstrates the power of intentional community and heart-centered living.

On **20 hectares of heartspace**, we are building a home for ourselves and our children to blossom, to restore peace, live in harmony, natural bliss and loving kindness.

We are creating a sustainable infrastructure that includes permaculture gardens, a forest school, eco-luxury accommodation, a retreat and healing center, a yoga and event space and a nature spa.

Essência is a nest where people share, sing, dance and laugh — where we live our optimal physical, mental and emotional health and experience our full potential.

### Our Services

As the Essência family, we are cocreating, holding and hosting our own **Essência signature retreats** and one festival per year.

We are weaving our gifts and talents together and birthing safe spaces for human beings to ground, center and celebrate life.

One of the pillars of Essência is our **Kidstemple Essência**. It is a place to come as parents and children together to support, unlearn, explore and play. We are in the progress of creating a forest & mystical school for our kids.

We also offer **individual services for healing, growth and expansion**, for example:

- energy & body work
- therapy
- embodiment
- yoga
- music classes

Every Thursday we celebrate our **Community Day** and open our doors for people from the region and visitors. On that day, we garden and practice together and enjoy our magical forest spa *(sauna, hottub & soup)*.

Another service in the making is **Essência TV** — a platform from which we share our wisdom, practices, community life, workshops and insights with the world.
