## Join Essência Tribe!
Sharing the Dream of a New Earth EcoVillage

A courageous experiment  
about coLiving off-grid in Harmony with Nature and each other

### Our Invitation
We invite you to co-create and support a future with Essência, a vibrant eco-village community dedicated to nurturing sustainable living, personal growth and harmony with the Earth

Nestled in a valley of the wild West Coast of the Algarve in Portugal, just 20 minutes from Aljezur  
Essência is a living embodiment of a harmonious relationship with nature and each other

At Essência, we are guided by the principles of permaculture, holistic living and collaborative community, offering

- A Living Sanctuary: diverse eco-living space nurtured by renewable energy and the bounty of our vegan, organic food
- Pathways to Flourishing: Transformative retreats, workshops and nourishing community gatherings that foster creativity and joy
- Conscious Community: A thriving social structure that demonstrates the power of intentional community and heart-centered living

On 20 hectares of heartspace, we are building a home for ourselves and our children to blossom  
to restore peace, live in harmony, natural bliss and loving kindness

We are creating a sustainable infrastructure that includes permaculture gardens, a forest school, eco-luxury accommodation, a retreat and healing center, a yoga and event space and nature spa

Essência is a nest where people share, sing, dance and laugh  
Where we live our optimal physical, mental and emotional health and experience our full potential

### Our Services
As the Essência family we are cocreating, holding and hosting our own Essência signature retreats and one festival per year  
We are weaving our gifts and talents together and birthing safe spaces for human beings to ground, center and celebrate life

One of the pillars of Essência is our Kidstemple Essência  
A place to come as parents and children together to support, unlearn, explore and play  
We are in the progress to create a forest & mystical school for our kids

We also offer individual services for healing, growth and expansion such as energy & body work, therapy, embodiment, yoga, music classes etc.

Every Thursday we celebrate our Community Day and open our doors for people from the region and visitors  
We garden and practice together and enjoy our magical forest spa (sauna, hottub & soup)

Another service in the making is Essência TV  
A platform to share wisdom, practices, community life, workshops and insights with the world
